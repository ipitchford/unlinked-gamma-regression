# Source verification — 20 September 2026

Bibliographic existence and contextual support were checked through primary sources. This is not an exhaustive priority audit.

- Balabdaoui, Slawski and Steffani, arXiv:2507.14986v1 (2025), https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.14986v1 : concluding minimality conjecture and Gamma convolution obstruction; target version fixed explicitly.
- Azadkia and Balabdaoui, JMLR 25(197):1–55 (2024), https://jmlr.org/papers/v25/22-0930.html : deconvolution least-squares estimation, identification and nonidentifiable solution-set formulation. Publisher page and PDF checked.
- Balabdaoui, Di Noia and Durot, JMLR 27(101):1–39 (2026), https://jmlr.org/papers/v27/25-0516.html : latent-law deconvolution under unlinked linear structure, nonunique coefficient set, assumption-specific Wasserstein rates. Publisher page and PDF checked; cite the published title rather than the earlier arXiv title.
- James, Roynette and Yor, Probability Surveys 5:346–415 (2008), DOI 10.1214/07-PS118 : classical positive Gamma convolution and Thorin measure context.
- Kunis, Peter, Römer and von der Ohe, Linear Algebra and its Applications 490:31–47 (2016), DOI 10.1016/j.laa.2015.10.023 : classical finite-atomic Prony reconstruction, not evidence that this manuscript invented the method.
- Akinshin, Goldman and Yomdin, arXiv:1701.04058v5 (2019; original 2017), https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.04058v5 : near-collision instability context, not a claimed stability theorem for this package.

The manuscript claims no transfer of rates to every Gamma configuration and no validated sample-cumulant estimator. Cited works are linked, not redistributed. Novelty and mathematical correctness remain separate assessments.
